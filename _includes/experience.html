{%- comment -%} Years of experience since site.author.career_start ("YYYY-MM"), rounded down to the nearest half year (e.g. 8.5). Calculated at build time; the script in the default layout recalculates it in the browser so it stays current between builds. Usage: {% include experience.html %} {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign start = site.author.career_start | split: "-" -%} {%- assign start_months = start[0] | times: 12 | plus: start[1] -%} {%- assign now_month = site.time | date: "%-m" -%} {%- assign now_months = site.time | date: "%Y" | times: 12 | plus: now_month -%} {%- assign half_years = now_months | minus: start_months | divided_by: 6 -%} {%- assign whole = half_years | divided_by: 2 -%} {%- assign half = half_years | modulo: 2 -%} {{ whole }}{% if half == 1 %}.5{% endif %}
